Questions people ask

What is free?

The first unit of every course, with its lessons, recall checks and end-of-unit practice exam. The readiness check and the Practice hub's quick drills are free too. On the courses that have no unlock yet, everything is open.

What does the unlock include?

Every remaining unit of that one course: all its lessons, all its timed practice exams and, where the course has them, its full-length mocks. It is a one-time payment for that course, good on up to three devices, with no renewal. Today Databricks DE Associate is $9.99 and Databricks DE Professional is $14.99; the other courses are open in full.

Does progress sync between the web and the apps?

No. Progress, mastery bars and readiness figures are stored in your browser's local storage, and the iOS and Android app has its own separate storage. There is no account to sync through, so the web and the app each keep their own progress. A purchase does carry over: enter your access code on the device you want to unlock.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Studying needs no login, and buying an unlock is a normal card checkout that gives you an access code, not an account. If you clear your browser data, your progress resets and your access code still works.

Study on your phone

The same courses, in the iOS and Android app. Free to start, no account, and an unlock you buy on the web works in the app with your access code.
