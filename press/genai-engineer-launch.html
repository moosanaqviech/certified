A Complete Path to the Databricks Generative AI Engineer Certification

Fifty-five interactive lessons and seven timed practice exams cover every objective of the official exam guide, as demand for production generative-AI skills climbs.

August 26, 2026. Certify today released a full preparation course for the Databricks Generative AI Engineer Associate certification, one of the first end-to-end study programs mapped to every objective of Databricks' current exam guide. The course is available now at certify.courses.

Generative AI has moved from experiment to production, and employers increasingly want verifiable proof that engineers can build and ship LLM-powered systems on the Databricks platform. Certify's new course prepares candidates for the exam and for the work behind it: designing retrieval-augmented generation pipelines, building and evaluating agents, and deploying governed models into production.

The course spans 55 lessons across six units and covers all 56 objectives of the official 18 March 2026 exam guide, paired with seven full-length timed practice exams that mirror the real 45-question format. Topics run the full Databricks generative-AI stack, including Mosaic AI Vector Search, the Mosaic AI Agent Framework, Foundation Model APIs, Mosaic AI Gateway, Agent Bricks, Genie Spaces, and model governance through Models in Unity Catalog.

Unlike static video courses, every lesson is a mobile-first, interactive walkthrough with built-in recall checks and animated explanations, designed to be studied in short sessions on a phone. The timed practice exams reproduce exam pressure so candidates walk in knowing what to expect.

Passing this exam should mean you can actually build these systems, not just recognize the right multiple-choice answer. We built all 55 lessons around what the exam tests and what the job demands, so candidates finish ready for both.
Moosa Naqvi, Technical Trainer, Certify

The Generative AI Engineer Associate course joins Certify's growing catalog, which already includes complete tracks for the Databricks Data Engineer Associate and Data Engineer Professional certifications, with AWS and Databricks Machine Learning courses in development. A full written guide to the exam is available on the Certify blog.

Availability and Pricing

The first unit of every course is free to start. The full Generative AI Engineer Associate course unlocks for a one-time $9.99, with access across up to three devices. Begin at the course home.
