AWS Machine Learning Engineer Associate (MLA-C01) Exam: Cost, Format and Question Types

The AWS Certified Machine Learning Engineer Associate (MLA-C01) exam costs $150 USD and gives you 130 minutes for 65 questions, 50 of which are scored. The passing score is 720 on a 100 to 1,000 scale, and the exam uses five question types: multiple choice, multiple response, ordering, matching, and case study.

The five question types, and why this exam has more of them

Most AWS associate exams use two question formats. MLA-C01 is one of the exams where AWS introduced a wider set, and the exam guide lists all five:

  • Multiple choice. One correct response and three distractors. The bulk of the paper.
  • Multiple response. Two or more correct responses out of five or more options, and the question states how many to select. Credit requires selecting every correct option and no incorrect one.
  • Ordering. A short list of responses you place in the correct sequence, such as the steps of a pipeline or workflow. The whole sequence must be right to score.
  • Matching. A set of responses paired against a list of prompts, for example services matched to the jobs they do. All pairs must be correct to score.
  • Case study. One scenario followed by two or more questions about it. Each question is scored separately, so a wrong answer on one does not sink the others.

The practical effect: ordering and matching punish half-knowledge harder than multiple choice does, because there is no partial credit and no way to reason backwards from four options. They reward having actually built and deployed a model, which fits how the whole exam is written.

How scoring works

Of the 65 questions, 15 are unscored items AWS is trialling for future exams. They are not identified while you sit the exam, so treat every question as scored.

Results come back as a scaled score from 100 to 1,000, with 720 as the minimum pass. That is a higher bar than the 700 AWS sets for its foundational exams, and it is not a percentage: scaling accounts for difficulty differences between exam forms. Scoring is compensatory, so you pass on the overall score rather than needing to clear each domain, and there is no penalty for a wrong answer, so nothing should be left blank.

Pacing is comfortable on paper: 130 minutes across 65 questions is exactly 2 minutes each. Case studies consume more than their share, so bank time on the straightforward multiple-choice items early.

FAQ

How much does the MLA-C01 exam cost?

The exam costs $150 USD, the standard AWS associate-level price, with local tax added at checkout in some countries. Each retake is paid in full.

How many questions are on the MLA-C01 exam and how long is it?

There are 65 questions and a 130-minute time limit, which averages exactly 2 minutes per question. Only 50 of the 65 are scored: the other 15 are unscored trial items that are not identified during the exam.

What question types does the MLA-C01 exam use?

Five types: multiple choice, multiple response, ordering, matching, and case study. Ordering and matching have no partial credit, and each question inside a case study is scored separately.

What is the passing score for MLA-C01?

The minimum passing score is 720 on a scaled range of 100 to 1,000, higher than the 700 used by AWS foundational exams. Scoring is compensatory, so you pass on your overall score rather than domain by domain.

Is MLA-C01 still available to take?

Yes, but the window is closing: the last day to take MLA-C01 in English is September 28, 2026, and registration for the updated MLA-C02 opens September 1, 2026. Confirm which version applies to your date before booking.
