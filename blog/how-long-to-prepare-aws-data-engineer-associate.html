How Long Does It Take to Prepare for the AWS Data Engineer Associate Exam?

Most candidates need 6 to 8 weeks of part-time study to pass the AWS Certified Data Engineer Associate (DEA-C01) exam. Engineers who already build pipelines on AWS often need only 3 to 4 weeks of review, while those new to AWS should budget 10 to 12 weeks. The exam guide assumes real experience going in.

Last updated August 2026.

Pick your timeline by experience

3 to 4 wk
Working AWS data engineer. Glue, Redshift, and Kinesis are part of your job already. You are closing gaps, not learning services.
6 to 8 wk
Some AWS exposure. Solid SQL and Python, comfortable with S3 and IAM, patchy elsewhere. The typical case.
10 to 12 wk
New to AWS. Coming from another cloud, on-premises ETL, or an analytics role.

These assume 6 to 8 hours a week, so the typical case lands near 40 to 60 hours total. DEA-C01 runs longer than a single-platform certification because of scope: the exam guide's target candidate has 2 to 3 years of data engineering experience and 1 to 2 years of hands-on AWS experience, and questions span ingestion, storage, operations, and security rather than one product.

Recommended experience is from the official DEA-C01 exam guide. It describes the target candidate, it is not an entry requirement.

Weight your weeks by domain, not by service count

DEA-C01 is not evenly split. Domain 1 alone is more than a third of the scored content, so an even four-way split underinvests in the part most likely to decide your result.

Domain weights from the official DEA-C01 exam guide. Full breakdown in the domains and weighting post.

Scoring is compensatory: no per-domain minimum, everything rolls into one scaled total against a passing score of 720. A strong domain can carry a soft one, but leaving Domain 4 as an afterthought is still a common way to fall short.

A concrete 8-week plan (the typical case)

The free course here is 36 chapters across the four official domains, and the chapter counts already track the weights: 13, 9, 8, and 6. That maps onto eight weeks with room for hands-on time and revision.

Weeks 1 to 2
Ingestion and transformation. Chapters 1 to 13: streaming versus batch, Kinesis Data Streams, Amazon Data Firehose, Amazon Managed Service for Apache Flink, AWS Glue, Redshift ELT, orchestration. Two weeks here is deliberate, this is 34% of the exam.
Week 3
Data stores. Chapters 14 to 22: Redshift versus DynamoDB versus RDS, data lakes on S3, the Glue Data Catalog, schema evolution, Apache Iceberg, lifecycle policies.
Week 4
Operations and support. Chapters 23 to 30: Athena, Redshift Spectrum, log analysis, data quality checks. Get CloudWatch versus CloudTrail airtight, it shows up constantly.
Week 5
Security and governance. Chapters 31 to 36: IAM authentication, IAM policies versus Lake Formation, KMS, Macie, audit logging. The smallest domain and the one most often skipped.
Week 6
Build something. Wire one pipeline end to end in your own account: S3 landing zone, a Glue crawler and job, an Athena query, a CloudWatch alarm. This turns memorized service names into recognizable scenarios.
Weeks 7 to 8
Diagnose, drill, book. Take the readiness quiz, re-read the chapters behind your two weakest domains, and work scenarios until you can say why the runner-up loses. Book when no domain trails badly.

Compress or stretch: on AWS daily? Skim Weeks 1 to 5 in two weeks, then spend the rest on Domain 4 and scenario practice. New to AWS? Double Weeks 1 to 5 and keep the hands-on week where it is.

What actually moves the timeline

  • Hands-on AWS time. The largest lever by a distance. The exam asks which service fits a constraint, and constraints are easier to recognize once you have hit them.
  • Breadth over depth. Deep Redshift expertise saves less time than you would expect, since Redshift is one of a dozen services in scope. Decision-level familiarity across all of them beats mastery of one.
  • Studying choices, not definitions. Knowing what Amazon Data Firehose does will not answer whether it beats Kinesis Data Streams for a given replay requirement.

How to tell the calendar is not lying to you

Weeks elapsed is a weak readiness signal. A better one is diagnostic: can you name your weakest domain and say why? The free DEA-C01 readiness quiz is 12 untimed scenario questions across all four domains, no signup, returning a per-domain readout that points at the units to redo. Run it after Week 5, then again before you book.

FAQ

How long does it take to prepare for the AWS Data Engineer Associate exam?

Most candidates need 6 to 8 weeks of part-time study, roughly 40 to 60 hours total. Engineers already working with AWS data services daily often need only 3 to 4 weeks of review, and candidates new to AWS should budget 10 to 12 weeks.

Can I pass the DEA-C01 in 30 days?

Yes, if you already build data pipelines on AWS and are mainly reviewing. Thirty days is enough to cover all four domains and drill scenario questions at that starting point. Coming in without hands-on AWS experience, 30 days is very tight and 10 to 12 weeks is the more realistic plan.

How many hours of study does the DEA-C01 need?

Plan for 40 to 60 hours if you have some AWS exposure, less if you work with these services daily and more if AWS is new to you. Spread across 6 to 8 weeks, that is about 6 to 8 hours a week.

Do I need 2 to 3 years of experience to take the DEA-C01?

No. The official exam guide describes its target candidate as having 2 to 3 years of data engineering experience and 1 to 2 years of hands-on AWS experience, but that is recommended background, not an eligibility rule. Anyone can register, and less experience mainly means a longer study runway.
