SAA-C03 Exam Cost, Format and Question Types

The AWS Solutions Architect Associate (SAA-C03) exam costs $150 USD and gives you 130 minutes for 65 questions, of which 50 are scored. Every question is either multiple choice (one correct answer of four) or multiple response (two or more of five or more), and you need a scaled score of 720 out of 1,000 to pass.

Last updated October 2026.

What SAA-C03 costs

The fee is $150 USD, the standard price for an AWS associate exam. AWS lists local-currency prices for some countries and updates prices at least once a year, so check the figure when you book. The price covers one attempt.

  • Retakes: if you fail, you must wait 14 calendar days before trying again. There is no limit on attempts, but each one is paid in full.
  • The 50% voucher: passing an AWS exam gives access to a 50% discount voucher for recertification or any other AWS exam. If SAA-C03 is not your first AWS certification, check whether you already hold one.
  • Validity: the certification lasts three years, after which you recertify.

Retake and voucher rules from the AWS Certification policies and benefits pages; fee and duration from the AWS certification page, checked 22 October 2026.

The two question types

Multiple choice

One correct answer and three distractors. On SAA-C03 the distractors are usually real AWS designs that would work, just not as well for the stated requirement. An option that is more expensive, more complex or less available than needed is wrong even though it is technically sound.

Multiple response

Two or more correct answers from five or more options, and the question says how many to pick. There is no partial credit: one wrong selection loses the whole question. Judge each option against the scenario on its own; do not pick a second answer because it seems to pair with the first.

No ordering, matching or case-study items. Some newer AWS exams, such as the AI Practitioner, add those formats. SAA-C03 uses only the two classic types, so practice on single-answer and multiple-response scenarios is practice on the real format.

How scoring works

Your raw result on the 50 scored questions is converted to a scaled score from 100 to 1,000, and 720 passes. The scaled score is not a percentage, so 720 does not mean 72% correct. AWS does not publish the exact conversion.

Scoring is compensatory: there is no pass mark per domain, only one for the whole exam. The score report can show how you did in each of the four domains, which is the useful part if you need to retake. There is also no penalty for guessing, so never leave a question blank.

Booking and test day

  1. Sign in to the AWS Certification account and choose the SAA-C03 exam.
  2. Choose a Pearson VUE test center or online proctored delivery, then pick a date and pay.
  3. At a test center, arrive 15 to 30 minutes early for sign-in. Online, you can launch the exam up to 30 minutes before the appointment to run the system and room checks.

Book a date before you feel completely ready: a fixed date keeps the study plan honest, and rescheduling is usually possible if you need more time.

FAQ

How much does the AWS Solutions Architect Associate exam cost?

$150 USD per attempt. Retakes are paid in full, and passing any AWS exam gives access to a 50% discount voucher for a later exam or recertification.

How many questions are on SAA-C03 and how long is it?

65 questions in 130 minutes. 50 are scored and 15 are unscored trial questions that you cannot tell apart from the rest.

Does SAA-C03 have partial credit on multiple-response questions?

No. A multiple-response question is only marked correct if every selection is correct, so one wrong pick loses the whole question.

How long do I have to wait to retake SAA-C03?

14 calendar days after a failed attempt. AWS sets no limit on the number of attempts, but each one costs the full exam fee.

Can I take the AWS Solutions Architect Associate exam online?

Yes. SAA-C03 can be taken online with a remote proctor or at a Pearson VUE test center. Online delivery needs a quiet private room and passes a system check before the exam starts.
