How to know when you are ready for the Spark Developer Associate

You are ready for the Databricks Certified Associate Developer for Apache Spark exam when you can write and read PySpark from memory across all seven exam sections, and you score 70 percent or better on timed, full-length mocks with no section far behind. Seventy percent is Certify's own practice target: Databricks publishes no passing score.

Last updated October 2026.

Readiness here means recall, not recognition

The exam is 45 scored multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes for USD 200, online proctored or at a test center, with every code snippet in Python. Unscored questions may also appear, unmarked. The detail that changes how you should judge readiness is in the official guide: no test aids, including API documentation. For the full format and the seven sections, see the complete certification guide.

Many questions show four code snippets where three use a real PySpark method with the wrong argument, the wrong order or the wrong mode. Feeling comfortable in a notebook with autocomplete is not the same signal. The honest test is whether you can say, unprompted, which of coalesce and repartition shuffles, or which save mode is the default, and why.

Format, cost, delivery and the no-test-aids rule are from the official Databricks exam guide (30 October 2025 edition) and certification page. The guide publishes no passing score.

What exam-ready looks like, by area

The readiness quiz groups the seven official sections into five areas. For each, the signal is a decision you can make without looking anything up. The weights are the summed official section weights.

The gaps that mean wait

  • Your material is from an older Spark. The course teaches Apache Spark 4.0. Study notes that say Koalas, call registerTempTable, or list Mesos as a cluster manager will teach answers the current exam does not use.
  • You recognize methods but cannot produce them. If you need to see the options before you know withColumnRenamed takes the old name first, the code-reading questions will cost you time and points.
  • You pass section exams but not timed mocks. Two minutes per question is tight when every snippet needs a careful read. Untimed scores overstate readiness.
  • One area is far behind. The DataFrame API alone is 30%, but the last area is 20% across three small sections that are easy to skip. Skipping them caps your score.

The tell: if you cannot name your weakest of the five areas and the chapter behind it, find that out before you book.

A faster way to find out than guessing

The free Spark Developer Associate readiness quiz is 12 questions across the five areas above, with no timer and no signup. At the end it shows how you scored in each area against how much that area weighs on the exam, and which units to study next. Each question targets one common mix-up, such as repartition versus coalesce or append versus complete output mode, so a miss names a specific gap.

It is a diagnostic, not a timed practice exam. Its job is to route you to the right unit of the course, not to simulate exam day.

The 70 percent target and when to book

Because Databricks publishes no cut score, treat 70 percent as our guidance, not an official line. A sequence that works:

  1. Section exams first. Score 70 percent or better on Practice Exams 1, 2 and 3 (Unit 1, Units 2 and 3, Units 4 to 7). Below that, work the flagged chapters before taking a mock.
  2. Then the mocks, timed. Practice Exams 4, 5 and 6 are 45 questions in 90 minutes, sampled at the official weights. Two of the three at 70 percent or better, with no area far behind, is a reasonable green light.
  3. Book one to two weeks out. That leaves time for the last mock and a review pass. The official exam guide asks candidates to check back for updates about two weeks before the exam, so reread it when you book.

If the readiness quiz shows gaps in the core, plan on the longer timelines in how long to prepare for the exam before booking anything.

FAQ

How do I know if I am ready for the Databricks Spark Developer Associate exam?

You are ready when you can write PySpark from memory across all seven sections and score 70 percent or better on timed full-length mocks with no area far behind. Seventy percent is Certify's practice target, since Databricks publishes no passing score.

What is the passing score for the Databricks Spark Developer Associate exam?

Databricks does not publish a passing score for this exam. Certify uses 70 percent as its own practice target, and you should confirm current details in the official exam guide when you register.

When should I book the Spark Developer Associate exam?

Book once you pass the section exams at 70 percent or better and are scoring there on timed mocks, then pick a date one to two weeks out. Reread the official exam guide when you book, since Databricks asks candidates to check it for updates about two weeks before the exam.

Is the Spark Developer Associate exam hard without the documentation?

It is harder than daily work suggests, because the exam allows no test aids, including API documentation. Many questions compare four code snippets that differ by one argument or method name, so recall of exact names and defaults matters.

Which section of the Spark Developer Associate exam should I study most?

The DataFrame API section, at 30 percent of the exam, followed by Architecture and Spark SQL at 20 percent each. Do not skip the small sections: Structured Streaming, Spark Connect and Pandas API on Spark add up to 20 percent together.
