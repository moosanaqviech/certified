AWS Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) Exam: Cost, Format and Question Types

The AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) exam costs $100 USD and gives you 90 minutes for 65 questions, 50 of which are scored. Questions are multiple choice and multiple response, the passing score is 700 on a 100 to 1,000 scale, and there are no prerequisites.

Question counts, question types, and the 700 passing score are stated in the official CLF-C02 exam guide. The exam guide does not cover duration, price, prerequisites, or delivery method: those come from the exam listing on the AWS Certification site. At $100 the Cloud Practitioner is the cheapest AWS exam: associate-level exams such as DEA-C01 are $150.

Why 65 questions but only 50 count

AWS includes 15 unscored questions in every CLF-C02 sitting. They are used to gather data on candidate performance for future exam versions, they are not identified while you take the exam, and they do not affect your result. Your 700 passing score is calculated from the 50 scored questions only.

Two things follow from that. First, the domain weightings you plan your studying around apply to the 50 scored questions, not the 65 you see. Second, an unusually strange or off-syllabus question is more likely a pilot item than a signal you missed a topic, so do not let one rattle you.

Sixty-five questions in 90 minutes works out to roughly 83 seconds each. Most foundational-level questions are short recall items you will answer in half that, which leaves comfortable margin to flag the handful that need thought and come back to them.

The two question types

CLF-C02 uses only two formats, and knowing the difference matters because they are scored differently in practice:

  • Multiple choice: one correct response and three distractors. The great majority of the exam.
  • Multiple response: two or more correct responses out of five or more options. The question states how many to select, and you must get all of them right. There is no partial credit.

There is no penalty for a wrong answer. An unanswered question and an incorrect one score identically, so never leave one blank. Use the review-and-flag feature to mark anything you are unsure of, answer it anyway with your best guess, and revisit it if time allows.

The exam guide is explicit about how the wrong answers are built: distractors are "generally plausible responses that match the content area." In practice that means a real AWS service that does a related job, so "which service stores objects" will have EBS and EFS sitting in the options. Service boundaries (what S3 is for versus EBS versus EFS) are worth more study time than service feature lists.

What the $100 actually covers

The fee is per attempt, before any applicable taxes, and it covers one sitting. If you do not pass, AWS requires a 14-day wait before you can retake the exam, and the retake is charged in full. There is no limit on the number of attempts, but each one is a fresh $100.

Two things worth checking before you pay. AWS periodically offers exam benefits to candidates who already hold a certification, and organizations in AWS partner or training programs often hold vouchers. Both are worth confirming on the AWS Certification site or with your employer, since neither is applied automatically at checkout.

How to register

  1. Create an AWS Certification account (it is separate from your AWS console account) and sign in.
  2. Select the Cloud Practitioner exam and confirm you are booking CLF-C02, the current version.
  3. Choose your delivery method: a Pearson VUE testing center or an online proctored session from home. The exam and the fee are identical either way.
  4. Pick a date and pay. Online proctored slots are typically available around the clock; testing center availability depends on your location.
  5. If you are testing online, run the system check for webcam, microphone, and connection well before exam day.
  6. Check in early with government-issued photo ID. For online sessions you will need a private room and a clear desk for the proctor's room scan.

You will normally see a provisional pass or fail result at the end of the session, with your official score report and digital badge following separately.

What the scored questions cover

The 50 scored questions are distributed across four domains: Cloud Concepts (24%), Security and Compliance (30%), Cloud Technology and Services (34%), and Billing, Pricing, and Support (12%). Scoring is compensatory, so you pass on your overall scaled score rather than domain by domain.

The practical read: Security and Compliance plus Cloud Technology and Services are 64% of the exam, so the shared responsibility model, IAM basics, and the core compute, storage, database, and networking services are where most of your preparation belongs. The full domain-by-domain breakdown is in the complete CLF-C02 certification guide.

FAQ

How much does the AWS Cloud Practitioner exam cost?

The CLF-C02 exam costs $100 USD per attempt, before any applicable taxes. That is the standard AWS foundational-level price, compared with $150 for associate-level exams.

How many questions are on the CLF-C02 exam and how long is it?

The exam has 65 questions and a 90-minute time limit. Only 50 questions are scored: the other 15 are unscored pilot items that are not identified during the exam and do not affect your result.

What is the passing score for the AWS Cloud Practitioner exam?

The minimum passing score is 700 on a scaled range of 100 to 1,000. AWS uses a compensatory scoring model, so you need to pass the exam overall rather than each domain separately.

What question types does CLF-C02 use?

Only two: multiple choice, with one correct response out of four, and multiple response, with two or more correct responses out of five or more. Multiple response questions have no partial credit, so you must select every correct option.

What happens if I fail the CLF-C02 exam?

You can retake it after a 14-day waiting period, and the retake costs the full $100 fee again. There is no cap on the number of attempts.
